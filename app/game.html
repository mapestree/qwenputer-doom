What happens on a turn

Your keys are written into the conversation as a move tool call. At </tool_call> the compiled CPU layers take over: the firmware (raycast.c) reads the keys from the tool-call buffer, moves you through E1M1's floor plan (from doom1.wad, 32 units per cell), casts 40 rays and prints the frame with putchar.

Every chip token is checked against a reference RV32 machine (“lock”); the game only advances on a clean run. Between turns the machine state is carried over and re-injected, the same way the driver loads any firmware.

Kept from E1M1: the floor plan and the exit switch. Not kept: heights, textures, monsters, items; doors are always open.
